# Commercial borrowing rates, 1945-2026

**Covers**: corporate bond yields by credit quality and bank borrowing costs for British
companies, 1945 to 2026, as the commercial rates a private transport company would pay. Parent:
[cost-of-capital](cost-of-capital.md). The sector and asset-finance evidence — hire purchase,
ship mortgages, aircraft finance — is in
[transport-finance-1945-2026](transport-finance-1945-2026.md).

## How to read the figures

- **Coupon** - the rate the instrument pays on its nominal amount.
- **Yield** - the return to a buyer at a given price. Coupon and yield are never substituted for
  each other.
- **Margin** - points added to a named reference rate (Bank Rate, LIBOR, swap rate, gilt).
- **Effective rate** - an average rate actually borne, as returned by lenders or borrowers.
- **Flat rate** - a hire-purchase convention in which interest is charged on the whole original
  advance for the whole term. It is not a true rate. *The Commercial Motor* stated the relation
  in 1974: the quoted flat rate was "in the order of 16.5 per cent so that the true rate is
  nearer 32 per cent" (C56). No conversion is applied here to any other flat rate.
- **Secured / unsecured** - each row states which. Asset-secured and unsecured lending are
  different prices for different risks and are kept in separate tables.
- **Primary / secondary** - primary means the originating document (company announcement,
  prospectus, statute, or an official compilation of primary prices). Secondary means a later
  work or a database restating the figure.

**Notes on Table 2**

1. C25 to C32 are averages over the whole corporate sector, dominated by large borrowers. The
   SME rows are the only evidence found of the size premium. In November 2025 the all-PNFC rate
   was 5.64 and the SME rate 6.18 (C46), a **computed** difference of 0.54.
2. The margin over Bank Rate is not constant. It is negative in 1955 (C26), about 0.9 in 1965
   (C27), and about 2.2 to 2.8 from 1990 onwards (C30, C32, C41-C45). The engine's present
   default of base plus 2 is inside the observed range for 1990-2026 but too high for 1945-1970.
3. C33 to C40 are an effective rate on all bank loans to companies, secured and unsecured
   together. They are not a mortgage rate and not an overdraft rate specifically.
4. The 1977 break flagged by the Bank means C33 and later are an effective rate on the whole
   PNFC sector, while C25 to C32 before 1977 are closer to a blue-chip lending rate.
