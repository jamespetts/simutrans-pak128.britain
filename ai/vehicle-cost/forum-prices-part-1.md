jamespetts, 29 December 2010. The forum paginates at 35 posts per page, so the page suffixes are
`.0`, `.35`, `.70`, `.105`, `.140`, `.175`, `.210`, `.245` and `.280`.
`[FORUM:https://forum.simutrans.com/index.php?topic=6521.0]`

Conventions, as extracted:

- Year is the build or purchase year as stated in the thread, not the year of the post.
- Price is exactly as stated. Shillings and pence are preserved as written, including the
  thread's own inconsistent notation. One pound is 20 shillings or 240 pence.
- Non-sterling figures are recorded in the currency stated and are not converted.
- Basis records what the thread says the figure is: build cost, contract price, estimate,
  second-hand price and so on. Where the thread divides a batch total into a per-vehicle figure,
  both the total and the per-vehicle figure are recorded, each in its own row.
- Source names the source as the thread names it, with a page reference where one is given.
- Post is the thread's own post number, with #0 the opening post.

Rows are numbered as in the extraction and are in the order the thread gives them. The division
between the two documents is at row 208 and is mechanical, not by vehicle class: the classes are
too interleaved in the thread for a classified split to be made safely by rule.
