## A10. Sefton and Weale, gross fixed capital formation, vehicles, ships and aircraft — constant prices

- **Identifier**: Sefton and Weale, table A14, series S051 "Vehicles Ships & Aircraft".
- **Units**: £ million at constant prices.
- **Coverage**: two published blocks, not one series:
  - 1920 to 1948 at 1938 prices (the sheet heading for this block is "1938 prices");
  - 1948 to 1990, no price base stated in the sheet.
- **URL fetched**: as A9 (sheet `GFCF KP`).
- **Access date**: 16 November 2026.
- **Publisher caveats**: the two blocks overlap in 1948 with different values (110 and 2,384),
  because they are on different price bases. They must not be spliced without a decision about the
  base. For the second block the workbook states no base; the 1985 constant-price value (6,440)
  is identical to the 1985 current-price value, which implies 1985 prices, but that is an inference
  from the figures, not a statement in the source.

## A11. Checked and not usable: Bank of England, A millennium of macroeconomic data

- **Identifier**: Bank of England, *A millennium of macroeconomic data*, version 3.1 (updated to
  2016), sheet `A47. Wages and prices`.
- **URL fetched**:
  https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/statistics/research-datasets/a-millennium-of-macroeconomic-data-for-the-uk.xlsx
  (27.5 MB), from https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/research-datasets
- **Access date**: 16 November 2026.
- **What the price sheet contains**: composite average weekly earnings (1913 = 100); CPI
  (2015 = 100); CPI annual inflation rate; CPI on the original method; an RPI-based index
  (2015 = 100); a CPI for Ireland (2015 = 100); a spliced wholesale/producer price index
  (2010 = 100); and an implied consumption deflator (2013 = 100).
- **Finding**: none of those series is specific to transport equipment, to vehicles, or to vehicle
  maintenance, and the wholesale/producer price index is economy-wide. The workbook's 109 sheets
  contain no asset breakdown of investment prices: sheets A11 and A12 give gross fixed capital
  formation at current prices and in volume measures for the whole economy only, and sheet A55
  splits the capital stock only into dwellings and non-dwellings.
- **Consequence**: no values are recorded from this workbook. It does not supply the long
  transport-equipment series that was hoped for.
