# Modern traction maintenance: multiple units

**Covers**: published maintenance costs for British electric and diesel multiple units and
passenger traction, with the scope and denominator of each figure. Parent:
[vehicle-cost-evidence](../vehicle-cost-evidence.md). Locomotives, wagons, the contractual
context and the scheduled/unscheduled question are in
[modern-traction-locomotives-and-wagons](modern-traction-locomotives-and-wagons.md).

## A. Multiple units and passenger traction

be refreshed. (RDG, *LTPRSS*, 6th edition, March 2018, para 146-147.)
