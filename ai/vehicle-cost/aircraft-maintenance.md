# Aircraft maintenance costs

**Covers**: published aircraft maintenance costs for British operators and international
benchmarks, with the scope and denominator of each figure. Parent:
[vehicle-cost-evidence](../vehicle-cost-evidence.md). The road vehicle figures are in
[road-maintenance-1959-2016](road-maintenance-1959-2016.md).

# Aircraft and road vehicle maintenance costs: published figures, 1930-2026

Compiled 2026-11-17. Evidence only. No conversion, no deflation, no derived ratio, no
estimation has been applied, and no figure in this file has been computed by the compiler.
Every figure is recorded in the unit the source states. Where a figure is a computed ratio in
the source itself, it is labelled **computed**. Where a source's category is not defined, that
is stated, and where a figure is a manufacturer's or analyst's target rather than an achieved
cost, that is stated in the row.

Abbreviations: d. = pence (pre-decimal unless stated); p. = new pence; mpw = miles per week;
FH = flight hour; FC = flight cycle; EFH = engine flight hour; EFC = engine flight cycle;
MH = man-hour; P/S = primary or secondary.

The existing file `vehicle-maintenance-sources.md` (sections 3, 4 and 6) already holds the
pre-1959 road figures and the United States A320 Form 41 averages. This file covers the two
gaps named in the brief and does not repeat those figures, except where a source states both
an earlier and a later value and the pair is needed to read the later one.
