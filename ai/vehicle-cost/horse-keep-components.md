# Horse keep costs: the components

**Covers**: published costs of feeding, shoeing, treating and stabling a working horse in Britain,
itemised by component. Parent: [vehicle-cost-evidence](../vehicle-cost-evidence.md). Totals,
working life, wages and the assessment are in
[horse-keep-totals](horse-keep-totals.md).
