# Kew archive visit plan: vehicle maintenance-cost gaps

maintenance-cost gaps in the cost-balancing work, what to capture from each, and the same for the
three other physical routes (library, Chasewater, HMRS). Gaps and workbook rows:
[cost-balancing-register](../cost-balancing-register.md) and

[cost-data-coverage](../cost-data-coverage.md); sources:
[research-pricing-report-39](research-pricing-report-39.md) (§2, §5) and

record and a claim tag per [claim-tags](../claim-tags.md); for archive documents the citation slot
of `[LITERATURE:...]` holds the document reference. Statements about what the checked-out files

**The 2874 method** (the label is not in the checked-out files `[UNVERIFIED]` as a name; the
method content is recorded in reports 20 §3.3, 36 §3.1/§5 and 40 §1.2/§1.8). Apply it per card:

1. Take one named engine's engine record card.
2. Transcribe: build cost (loco; with tender); every works entry — date, works, repair grade as
   written on the card; cost of each works entry where given; every boiler exchange with the
   locomotive mileage at the exchange; any new-component prices (e.g. new boiler).
3. Map grade wording to the engine-history codes (GWR G/H/I/L; BR adds HG/HI/LI/C/U) and to the
   heavy/light definitions of Fowler 1913 (report 40 §1.2: heavy = re-boilered, or boiler out for
   general repair, or new tyres on 4+ wheels, or any two of {new cylinders; new axles; re-tubing;
   wheels turned plus boxes refitted or motion/brake overhauled; in-frame boiler repair with 15%+
   stays renewed}; light = any single item).
4. Derive per engine: the works ladder from new (2874 corrected reading: Intermediate year 2
   (22 months), Heavy Overhaul year 6.5, Heavy General year 13-14; report 40 §1.8); each grade's
   cost as % of build cost (2874: heavy general £450-700 = 9-14% of £4,992); boiler-exchange
   mileage (2874: mean 83,901 miles over 15 exchanges, 1,258,517 miles / 44.5 years); component
   shares (boiler £971 = 19.5% of £4,992; King boiler £1,687 of £6,172 = 27.3%).
5. Transfer **ratios, not pound levels** (report 36 §8). The 2874 card's earlier published reading
   was corrected (report 40 §1.8) — read the card's own wording and re-derive.
6. Sample: one card per archetype (express pacific; express 4-4-0/Victorian; mixed traffic 4-6-0;
   heavy freight 2-8-0; suburban tanks) to replace the archetype estimates in the `Overhauls &
   upgrades` sheet's per-class steam cells.

Capture: photograph each card whole plus close-ups of every works entry line; ledger pages
photographed; only card headers transcribed in the room. Gap closed: per-class steam overhaul
interval and share rows; Mk 1 outturn rows (RAIL works cost records); the per-train-mile cost
series route for the steam fixed/per-km split (60/40) split ([maintenance-decomposition](../vehicle-cost/maintenance-decomposition.md)
"Rail locomotives").
