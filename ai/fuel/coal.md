# Fuel prices: coal and gas

**Covers**: British coal, coal gas and producer gas prices, 1700s to 2026, as published, with the
basis and district of each figure. Parent: [fuel-evidence](../fuel-evidence.md). Oil, petrol,
electricity, marine bunkers and aviation fuel are in
[oil-and-power](oil-and-power.md).

## 1. Coal

The most valuable series located is Clark and Jacks's pithead series for the northeast coalfield,
which runs by decade from the 1700s to the 1860s. It is the only series found that reaches back
before 1770 with a stated method and a stated level.

The full column runs 1700s 2.31, 1710s 2.25, 1720s 2.46, 1730s 2.47, 1740s 2.34, 1760s 3.07,
1780s 3.08, 1820s 5.31 and 1840s 4.28, so the series is continuous by decade from the 1700s.

Method as stated by the source: prices from printed and archival sources for Newcastle and
individual collieries 1730-1869 were combined in a regression with a locational coefficient per
coal type and a year dummy; the result was linked on export prices to Church's northeast pithead
prices for 1882 onwards. The standard error of each decade relative to the 1860s is stated as
typically 5 to 8 per cent. The series is quality-controlled: the neighbouring columns of the same
table show Wallsend at 4.45, 8.27 and 8.75 shillings in the 1750s, 1850s and 1860s against the
averages above.

## 2. Coal gas, town gas and producer gas

No British town gas price per therm or per thousand cubic feet was located for any year before
1950\. See the Assessment.

| Year | Fuel | Price as stated | Unit | Basis | Region | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1950-51 | Gas, average net selling value | 6.17 | pence per therm | DOM | Great Britain | S7 | P |
| 1960-61 | Gas, average net selling value | 10.75 | pence per therm | DOM | Great Britain | S7 | P |
| 1977-78 | Gas, average net selling value | 18.50 | pence per therm | DOM | Great Britain | S7 | P |
| 1961-62 | Gas, average realised price | 21.8 | pence per therm | All gas sold | England and Wales | S10 | P |
| 1974 (Mar) | Gas, industrial tariff | 9.06 | pence per therm | Industrial tariff | Great Britain | S11 | P |
| 1975 (Mar) | Gas, industrial tariff | 11.00 | pence per therm | Industrial tariff | Great Britain | S11 | P |
| 1993 | Gas, average | 0.769 | pence per kWh | DELIV-IND | Great Britain | S8 | P |
| 2025 | Gas, average | 3.640 | pence per kWh | DELIV-IND | Great Britain | S8 | P |

The 1968 return gives the all-gas-sold price for England and Wales as 22.1 pence per therm in
1962-63, 22.3 in 1963-64, 22.4 in 1964-65, 22.1 in 1965-66 and 22.2 in 1966-67, against 26.0, 27.4,
26.6, 25.4, 25.9 and 27.8 pence per therm for the Scottish Gas Board.

The 1979 return gives the domestic net selling value for 1959-60 (10.38), 1969-70 (10.70),
1970-71 (10.58), 1974-75 (11.63), 1975-76 (14.35) and 1976-77 (16.65). A 1976 answer gives
12.9 pence per therm for domestic consumers and 10.4 pence per therm for commercial consumers in
calendar 1975.

**Basis warning.** The domestic net selling value (6.17 to 18.50 pence per therm) is revenue divided
by quantity sold to domestic consumers. The all-gas-sold figure for England and Wales (21.8 to 22.2
pence per therm) is roughly double it because it includes industrial and commercial sales. The two
are not comparable and must not be spliced.

No producer gas price was located. Producer gas was made and burned on the works site and was not
normally sold, which is consistent with the absence of a market price for it.
